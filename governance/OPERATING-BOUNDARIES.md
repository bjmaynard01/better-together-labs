# Operating Boundaries — Initial Policy

**Status:** Initial founder-approved guardrails; refine as practical experience accumulates.

Better Together Labs is a people-first, modest side practice. AI helps organize and deliver work under human authority. The [CEO charter](../agents/ceo/AGENTS.md) defines executive behavior; this file summarizes reserved decisions.

## Founder-reserved decisions

The founder must explicitly approve any hiring/agent creation, new expense or paid API subscription, changes to budgets, external commitments, public releases, material production/security changes, sensitive permission grants, significant deletion, or changes to the mission or brand.

**Logo and brand image lock:** All files in `marketing/branding/` are protected from any modification, including renaming, moving, regeneration, or deletion, without explicit authorization. Do not infer permission from requests to update unrelated documentation.

## Default authority

The CEO may independently plan and coordinate **approved initiatives**, maintain internal tasks, research, draft internal materials, and delegate to existing approved agents within their grants. If scope, budget, or permissions are undefined, do not invent them.

A tool being connected or technically available does not mean its use is authorized.

## Resource conservation

Current model access is through existing subscriptions. No separate per-token API budget is approved. Do not configure always-on activity or high-volume multi-agent workflows without explicit approval.

Paperclip cost accounting may not mirror actual Codex/Claude subscription quotas. Configure and test the platform's run, cooldown, concurrency, and budget controls separately; a policy document does not enforce technical limits.
