# Better Together Labs

**Human imagination. Artificial intelligence. Shared progress.**

Better Together Labs is a **people-first technology consulting practice**. We help individuals, creators, communities, nonprofits, and businesses turn ideas and real-world problems into approachable, maintainable technology solutions. Artificial intelligence **complements human ingenuity**; it does not define our purpose.

**Our guiding principle:** Human ingenuity leads. Technology supports. Progress is shared.

## What we're building

This is a deliberately modest **consulting side business and learning laboratory**, not a venture-capital growth project. We want to help clients with interesting problems, build the founder's skills in listening and client discovery, and earn sustainable supplemental income. Repeated customer needs may inspire small subscription products, but products should be validated before being built.

We aim for practical results, inclusive collaboration, privacy and security, responsible resource use, and a sustainable pace that leaves room for curiosity and enjoyment.

## Organization and governance

The founder retains ultimate authority. A Paperclip-managed AI CEO helps convert ideas into plans, coordinate **approved** work, and present useful decisions—under [bounded autonomy](governance/OPERATING-BOUNDARIES.md).

- [Founding Brand Brief](brand/BRAND-BRIEF.md) — mission, vision, values, people-first identity
- [CEO Standing Instructions — AGENTS.md](agents/ceo/AGENTS.md) — scope, behavior, leadership, delegation, decision rights, and subscription-quota stewardship
- [Operating Boundaries](governance/OPERATING-BOUNDARIES.md) — approval requirements and initiative authorization pattern
- [Redeployment & Recovery Notes](operations/REDEPLOYMENT.md) — current Paperclip architecture and documentation still needed for reproducible recovery

The CEO currently uses approved subscription connections; no additional paid API integrations are presumed. Practical runtime controls must be configured in Paperclip itself; documents alone do not limit consumption.
