# Better Together Labs

**Human imagination. Artificial intelligence. Shared progress.**

Better Together Labs is a **people-first technology consulting organization**. We help individuals, creators, communities, and businesses make technology work for their ideas, with AI complementing—not displacing—human ingenuity.

**Our guiding principle:** Human ingenuity leads. Technology supports. Progress is shared.

## Founding direction

Read the [Founding Brand Brief](brand/BRAND-BRIEF.md) for our mission, vision, principles, values, and early identity direction.

## Branding explorations

Our [branding library](marketing/branding/README.md) now includes **six original generated PNG logo concepts** exploring a Tree of Life silhouette, a subtle rainbow ring, and our people-first philosophy.

The original PNGs are the preferred visual references. The separate programmatically generated SVGs are **superseded experiments**, not approved logo files. No final identity has been selected yet.

## What comes next

- Choose and refine a master logo from the uploaded concepts.
- Build a consistent system of primary, horizontal, symbol-only, small-format, and monochrome assets.
- Produce faithful, production-ready vector files with accessible variants.
- Translate our founding commitments into governance and operating principles.

We believe technology should make life better—and that the best way forward is together.
